1. Who is responsible

ipe.rest is operated by Davy Kim (ipe Studio), Seoul, as an individual. Questions about this policy or your information: ipe.haven@gmail.com.

2. Where this policy applies

This policy covers ipe.rest and the subdomains run by the same operator: idle.ipe.rest, still.ipe.rest, draft.ipe.rest, iltonggi.ipe.rest, inkisle.ipe.rest, pawstcard.ipe.rest and play.ipe.rest, plus any other *.ipe.rest site we add later (together, “the Sites”). The mobile apps are separate: each app has its own privacy policy, which controls for that app. If a Site has its own privacy page with more specific details, read both; on cookies, analytics and advertising on the Sites, this page is the general rule and the more specific page adds to it.

3. What we collect

Visit statistics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read. It records page views, the page you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate location (country or city) and clicks on links that leave our site (the link address only). It sets cookies in your browser (such as _ga, which can last up to two years) to tell visits apart. We do not ask for your name or email in analytics, and we cannot identify you from it. Google processes this data on our behalf, which can include servers outside Korea.

Visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

The home page also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused service that does not use cookies or track individual visitors across sites. It measures aggregate page views and page loading performance.

Redirect pages

Some links shared by the studio pass through a short redirect page before opening YouTube. These pages record which track code was opened and a rough source (app, social, other). They contain no personal text.

Stored in your browser

When you pick a language we save that choice in your browser’s local storage under the name ipe_lang. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. The site also keeps a cache of its own files so it can open quickly and work offline. Your cookie choices are stored by the consent message (see section 6). You can clear all of this in your browser settings.

Email you send us

If you write to ipe.haven@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply and to handle your request, and we keep it as long as needed for that. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Hosting

The Sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, it receives technical request data such as your IP address; GitHub’s own privacy statement applies to that.

4. How we use it

To understand what is read and improve the Sites, to show advertising that helps pay for them, to reply to your messages, and to keep the Sites working and secure. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who else sees it

Google (analytics and advertising), Cloudflare (analytics) and GitHub (hosting) as described in this policy. Advertising partners that take part in Google’s ad systems may also receive technical data from your browser when ads load (see section 7). Links to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud, X and Facebook lead to services with their own policies. We do not share your information with anyone else except where the law requires.

6. Cookies and consent

The Sites use these kinds of cookies and similar storage:

  • Analytics — Google Analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_* (up to two years). Used to count visits and see which pages are read.
  • Advertising — where ads are shown, cookies and identifiers set by Google and its advertising partners to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and, if you allow it, personalise them.
  • Preferences — your language choice and your cookie choice, stored in your browser.
  • Strictly necessary — the offline cache of the Sites’ own files.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through a Google-certified consent message before analytics or advertising cookies are set, and the Sites work the same if you decline. We use Google Consent Mode: until you agree, Google’s tags do not store cookies for analytics or advertising and may send only limited, cookie-free signals used for aggregate measurement. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy settings option shown on the Sites, or by clearing the site’s cookies and storage in your browser.

7. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The Sites may show advertising served by Google AdSense. At the moment we plan to show ads only on ipe.rest and idle.ipe.rest; the other Sites show no advertising, and we will update this policy before that changes.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to our Sites or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Sites and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info (in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu).
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We choose which ad slots appear, not which ads: Google and its partners decide that. We do not give advertisers your personal information, and we do not place ads on the contact or privacy pages.

8. Your choices

  • Use the consent message or privacy settings option on the Sites, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The Sites work the same without cookies.
  • Turn off ad personalisation as described in section 7.
  • Clear site data to remove your saved language, your cookie choice and the offline cache.

9. Access, deletion and your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to restrict how we use it. Write to ipe.haven@gmail.com from the address you used. We reply within 10 days. Analytics data is not tied to your name or email, so we usually cannot find an individual in it; email correspondence we can locate and delete. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the rights set out in the GDPR or UK GDPR, including to object, to withdraw consent at any time, to data portability and to complain to your local data protection authority. Where we rely on your consent (analytics and advertising cookies), that is our legal basis; for replying to your email it is our legitimate interest in answering you.

10. Children

The Sites are not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. We do not knowingly show personalised ads to children.

11. Complaints

If you are in Korea, you can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

12. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Current version: 2026-11-06. See also the terms of use.

방문 통계 (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

5. 제공·위탁

이 방침에서 밝힌 Google(통계·광고), Cloudflare(통계), GitHub(호스팅)가 처리에 관여합니다. Google 광고 시스템에 참여하는 광고 파트너도 광고가 로드될 때 이용자 브라우저의 기술 정보를 받을 수 있습니다(7항 참조). YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud, X, Facebook 으로 연결되는 링크는 각 서비스의 방침을 따릅니다. 법령에 따라 요구되는 경우를 제외하고 그 외 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다.

6. 쿠키와 동의

사이트는 다음 종류의 쿠키와 유사한 저장 기술을 사용합니다.

  • 분석 — _ga, _ga_* 같은 Google Analytics 쿠키(최대 2년). 방문 수를 세고 어떤 페이지가 읽히는지 보는 데 씁니다.
  • 광고 — 광고를 게재하는 경우, Google 과 광고 파트너가 광고를 전달하고, 노출 빈도를 제한하고, 성과를 측정하고, 허용하신 경우 맞춤화하기 위해 설정하는 쿠키·식별자.
  • 환경설정 — 브라우저에 저장되는 언어 선택과 쿠키 선택.
  • 필수 — 사이트 자체 파일의 오프라인 캐시.

유럽경제지역, 영국, 스위스에서는 분석·광고 쿠키를 저장하기 전에 Google 인증을 받은 동의 안내로 동의를 구하며, 거부하셔도 사이트는 똑같이 동작합니다. Google 동의 모드(Consent Mode)를 사용합니다. 동의하시기 전까지 Google 태그는 분석·광고용 쿠키를 저장하지 않으며, 합산 측정에 쓰이는 제한적이고 쿠키 없는 신호만 보낼 수 있습니다. 사이트에 표시되는 개인정보 설정 항목이나 브라우저에서 사이트 쿠키·저장소를 지우는 방법으로 언제든 선택을 바꾸거나 철회하실 수 있습니다.

7. 광고 (Google AdSense)

사이트에는 Google AdSense 가 제공하는 광고가 게재될 수 있습니다. 현재는 ipe.rest 와 idle.ipe.rest 에만 광고를 게재할 계획이며, 그 밖의 사이트에는 광고가 없고 이 점이 바뀌기 전에 이 방침을 먼저 고치겠습니다.

  • Google 을 포함한 제3자 공급업체는 이용자가 저희 사이트나 다른 웹사이트를 이전에 방문한 기록을 바탕으로 광고를 게재하기 위해 쿠키를 사용합니다.
  • Google 의 광고 쿠키 사용으로 Google 과 파트너는 저희 사이트 및/또는 인터넷의 다른 사이트 방문을 바탕으로 이용자에게 광고를 게재할 수 있습니다.
  • Google 광고 설정에서 맞춤 광고를 끄거나, aboutads.info(유럽은 youronlinechoices.eu)에서 일부 제3자 공급업체의 맞춤 광고용 쿠키 사용을 거부하실 수 있습니다.
  • Google 서비스를 쓰는 사이트의 정보를 Google 이 어떻게 사용하는지는 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites 에 설명되어 있습니다.

저희는 광고 자리를 정할 뿐 어떤 광고가 나올지는 Google 과 파트너가 정합니다. 광고주에게 이용자의 개인정보를 제공하지 않으며, 문의·개인정보 페이지에는 광고를 두지 않습니다.
