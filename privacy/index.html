ipe.rest (the "Site")
{{OPERATOR_NAME}} (the "Operator") publishes this policy to comply with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. The Site has no sign-up, no payments and no advertising. The only personal information the Operator receives directly is what you include when you email us. A visit-statistics tool also collects some information automatically; all of it is described below.

01What we process, and why

  • The Operator does not ask for your national ID number, phone number, address or payment details, and does not collect sensitive information.
  • The Site shows no ads and sets no cookies for advertising.
  • What you write in an email is used only to reply to you.

02Tools the Site uses

  1. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — usage statistics such as visits, page use and link clicks. It uses cookies (_ga, _ga_*). ipe.rest and the music site idle.ipe.rest share the same GA4 property. Provider: Google LLC (including affiliates such as Google Ireland Limited).
  2. Cloudflare Web Analytics — counts page views and performance metrics without cookies. Provider: Cloudflare, Inc.
  3. GitHub Pages — stores and serves the Site's files. GitHub, Inc. may process server access logs (including IP addresses) for security and operations.
  4. Browser storage — your choice of language (ipe_lang) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the Operator's server and you can clear it in your browser. A service worker also keeps page and icon files on your device so the Site can open offline.

External links on the Site (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud, X and others) take you to those services, and what happens to your data after that follows their policies. The music site idle.ipe.rest has its own privacy policy.

03Cookies and similar technologies

On this Site, Google Analytics uses cookies. You can allow, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The Site works with cookies blocked, though your language choice may not be remembered. You can also opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

04Disclosure to third parties, processors, and transfers abroad

The Operator does not sell your personal information or provide it to third parties for payment, and does not disclose it without your consent except where the law requires. The services below communicate directly with your browser from overseas servers and process the automatically collected information in Section 1. Transfers happen automatically over the network when you visit the Site.

05Retention and deletion

  • Inquiry emails: deleted promptly once 1 year has passed after the inquiry is resolved, by permanent deletion from the mailbox.
  • Information held by analytics tools follows each provider's retention period (Section 4).
  • Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been fulfilled is destroyed in a way that cannot be recovered. Information that must be kept by law is stored separately and destroyed when that period ends.

06Your rights and how to use them

You may ask the Operator at any time to give you access to, correct or delete your personal information, to stop processing it, or to withdraw your consent. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will handle it without delay and tell you the result. A representative may make the request on your behalf. You can refuse cookies yourself as described in Section 3. Information that analytics tools collect only through cookie identifiers cannot be tied to you by the Operator, so access and deletion requests may be hard to fulfil; in that case we will point you to the provider's own user tools (such as Google's).

07Children under 14

The Site is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please tell us by email and we will delete it right away.

08Security measures

The Operator keeps inquiry emails in an account only the Operator can access and applies additional authentication to the account. Personal information is handled only as far as needed and is not shared outside.

11Rights for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Users in these regions may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent, and may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Make a request through the contact in Section 6.

12Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we post the change at the top of this page from 7 days before it takes effect, and from 30 days before for important changes that are unfavourable to users.
