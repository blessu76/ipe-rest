About ipe()

스튜디오 소개

A small independent studio in Seoul, making quiet music and private apps for people who work a little too long.

Who we are

ipe() (pronounced “eye-puh”) is a small one-person studio in Seoul, run by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}. The name comes from idle process: the state a processor enters when it has finished its work and simply rests. We make for that moment.

No noisy dashboards, no cloud listening in. The job is to make a little space where a tired mind can exhale. We believe technology can ask for less of you, so we design calmly and keep privacy as the default.

The studio began in 2026 as a habit of making calm things after long days of writing software, and it is still deliberately small. Everything published on ipe.rest is written, composed, designed and built by the studio.

What we make

Four products today, each started from the same question: could this feel a little more human?

  • idle()AI lo-fi and healing music for resting. Live on YouTube, and at idle.ipe.rest.
  • still()On-device bible comfort. Early access.
  • draft()On-device journaling that turns a few words into a story. Early access.
  • pawstcardPet visit reports for sitters and dog walkers. In development.

How we work

Calm by design

No streaks, no badges, no notifications that exist only to bring you back. If a screen does not help you rest or finish, it does not ship.

Private by default

Where a feature can run on your device, it does. Where it cannot, we say so plainly and keep what we collect to the minimum. The details live in our Privacy Policy.

Small on purpose

We would rather make four things well than forty things loudly. That also means we answer our own mail.

Where the name comes from

An idle process is what a computer runs when it has nothing left to do. We borrowed the idea for the moment a long day finally ends. ipe() is a software and music studio, and is not related to ipe (ipê) hardwood, lumber or decking.

About this site

ipe.rest is the studio’s home. It shows no ads and uses a visit-statistics tool only. Details are in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using the site are in the Terms of Use.
