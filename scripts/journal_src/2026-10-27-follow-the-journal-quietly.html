Follow the journal, quietly

저널을 조용히 따라가세요

The ipe() journal now has an RSS feed: follow new entries in the reader of your choice, with no account and no sign-up.

RSS is an old, plain idea: a site publishes the list of its newest entries as a small file, and your reader checks it when you ask it to. No account, no algorithm deciding what you see, no notification that interrupts you.

Our feed lives at https://ipe.rest/journal/feed.xml. It lists the entries in English, newest first, and only those that are already published. Journal pages also announce it in their head, so most readers can find it if you simply paste ipe.rest/journal/.

The entries are written in English and Korean, and each is available in 12 languages on the site. The feed carries the English title and summary and links to the English page; the other languages are one click away from each entry.

It fits how we like to work: a quiet thing that waits to be read. Open it when you have a minute.
