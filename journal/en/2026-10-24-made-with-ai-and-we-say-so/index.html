Made with AI, and we say so.

Every ipe() track is made with AI, and we say so on the page, not in the fine print.

The music is produced with AI (Suno). The lyrics and the concept begin as an AI draft; a person then revises it, reviews it and decides what stays. The idea behind each series, the order of a record, the final yes or no on a track — those decisions are ours.

Why say it out loud? Because this music is meant for tired hours: the ride home after a late night, the evening you can’t switch off. Whoever presses play then is trusting it a little, and that trust shouldn’t depend on a guess about how it was made.

Saying so is not a claim that the music is better or worse than anything else, and it doesn’t make the listening less real. The ride home is still yours. It is simply an honest label, like the ingredient list on a jar.
